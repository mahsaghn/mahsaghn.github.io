Mahsa Ghaderan is a PhD student at the Max Planck Institute for Informatics and Saarland University. Previously, she earned her master’s degree from Sharif University of Technology in Tehran, graduating as the top-ranked student in her class. She received her bachelor’s degree from Iran University of Science and Technology, where she also graduated as the top-ranked student. Her research lies at the intersection of computer networking and societal studies, with a particular focus on understanding human behavior and social dynamics through digital traces.
